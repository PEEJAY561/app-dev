## 🧠 Overview
**Stranger Things** is a *sci-fi horror* series created by the Duffer Brothers and released on **Netflix**.  
It’s set in the 1980s in the small town of **Hawkins, Indiana**, where strange supernatural events begin after a young boy goes missing.

---

## 🎭 Main Characters
- **Eleven** – A girl with psychokinetic powers.  
- **Mike Wheeler** – Loyal friend and group leader.  
- **Jim Hopper** – The town’s chief of police.  
- **Joyce Byers** – Determined mother searching for her son.  

---

## ⚡ Why I Love It
1. **’80s Nostalgia:** From music to fashion, it captures the era perfectly.  
2. **Character Growth:** Each season deepens emotional connections.  
3. **Mystery + Horror Blend:** Balances suspense, heart, and humor.  

---

## 🔮 Favorite Episode
### *Season 1, Episode 8: "The Upside Down"*
The finale perfectly ties the mystery together while leaving us questioning what’s next.
